# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all participants.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and inclusive
- Maintain sportsmanship
- Report security issues responsibly
- Give credit to others
- Help fellow participants learn

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Cheating or fraud
- Attacking competition infrastructure
- Sharing solutions during competition
- Harassment or discrimination
- Unauthorized access
- Malicious behavior

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- Disqualification
- Permanent ban
- Legal action (if applicable)
- Public reporting

## Reporting

Report violations to organizers immediately.

By participating, you agree to this Code of Conduct.
